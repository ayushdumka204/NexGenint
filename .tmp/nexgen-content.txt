NexGen QMS (Quality Management System)
Trust the Insight Because You Can Trust the Data
Quality Philosophy
Quality is not a final check. It is a research process. NexGen QMS is our structured research quality-management framework designed to identify data-quality risks throughout fieldwork.
Six Quality Layers
01 Respondent Verification — Identity, screening criteria and quota eligibility.
02 Location Validation — Interview location and interviewer movement checks where methodology permits.
03 Duration Analysis — Interview length, interviewer productivity and time gaps.
04 Recording Review — Interview recordings reviewed where consent and study design permit.
05 Response Quality — Straight-lining, speeding, inconsistent responses, weak open ends and suspicious patterns.
06 Data Consistency — Cross-question logic, quota consistency and anomaly detection.
Principle
Authentic Respondents → Authentic Data → Credible Insights → Better Decisions
